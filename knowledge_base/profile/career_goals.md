# Career Goals

## Overall Career Direction

My goal is to build a career at the intersection of software engineering, machine learning, artificial intelligence, and data science. While having fun.

I am particularly interested in applying technical skills to real-world problems and building systems that are useful beyond a purely a cool factor.

## Short-Term Goals

In the early stages of my career, I want to gain experiencing working on intrinsically valuable system application and learn from experienced engineers, data scientists, and machine learning practitioners.

I want to strengthen my understanding of how machine learning and AI systems are developed in professional environments, including:

Data collection and preparation
Model development
Model evaluation
A/B Testing
Deployment
Monitoring
Software engineering practices
Experimentation
Communication of technical results

I am especially interested in opportunities where I can contribute to an existing team while continuing to develop my technical depth.

## Areas of Interest

Areas Of Interest:

Machine Learning
Computer Vision
Generative AI
Data Science
Software Development

## Long-Term Goals

Long-term I would like to be technically strong and capable at building all types of systems but specifically AI and machine learning applications.

At the moment I eventually want to be able to take my masters degree in some form of computer science. I really want to create applications that help the everyday person in day to day activities or just improve quality of life overall.

I am also interested in eventually working on changing how we define AI usefulness, from a token to value system but to more of a computation / resources used value system. I think this is the only way to keep AI scaling sustainable.
