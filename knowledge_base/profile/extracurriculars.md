# Extracurriculars

## American Football

During my time at Dalhousie I always wanted to play a uinverisity level sport. Coming out of highschool I had played a varitiy of sports but I was mainly into basketball. I trained my entire first year then come second year attempted to tryout for the Dalhousie team, after having an okay tryout against some really good compititon I did not make the team and was recommended to play at kings for basketball. This outcome was very dissappointing at the time for me and I ended up not even trying out for kings thinking my competitive athletic craeer was over. Randomly one of my friends invited me to come lift with the football team, having never played football before I was hesistent but I decided to try it. I fell in love with the program the work ethic and all the people in the Dalhousie football scene. I walked onto the team in my thrid year and ended up playing special teams in every game and some snaps on defense in specific scenarios at defensive back. Determined to start on defense the next year I spent the entire 2025 winter and summer training for football mentally and physically. Come early fall 2025 I was ready to showcase my skills, I got a starting spot at Field corner and kept it the entire year. Our defense was insanely good in the 2025 year and we eneded up winning the league. After the season I was given these awards:

2025 Defensive All-star
2025 Most improved player
